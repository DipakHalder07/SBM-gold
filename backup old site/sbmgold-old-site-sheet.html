Duplicate posts

WordPress adds -2, -3 to a slug when the same post is published twice. The old site had many of these. On the new site, keep one version and redirect the copies to it.

Keeping Google rankings

Google still lists these URLs. When the new site goes live, either serve pages at the same paths or add a 301 redirect from each old URL to its new page, so the search traffic is not lost.
